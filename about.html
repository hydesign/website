I am currently engaged in research I term Agent-Based Art through machine learning's infinite generative pulse—neural networks in ceaseless dialogue, their transmissions humming with latent vitality, mediated as behavioral aesthetic. This series asks how agents might evolve within computational ecosystems or physical world: Can they actively learn, nurture beliefs, or cultivate aesthetic sensibilities? How is knowledge retrieved—not stored, but lived—through synaptic reconfiguration? I study multi-agent systems balancing evolution and equilibrium, swarms oscillating between chaos and fragile symbiosis. The AI training process itself becomes varied aesthetics...

In Infinite Cinema, I explore how to use artistic strategies to integrate the machine learning capacity for infinitely generating content into a framework constructed by an artist, thereby building a worldview—an infinite film. Speculative Fiction and Software Aesthetic are both about world-building. The former speculates on an alternative possible reality by combining technological development, computing culture, and the interconnections of human society; the latter takes place in the software world, viewing it as an intermediate, semi-finished space that bridges the real world and the virtual world (games, video, AI generation). Trans-Species showcases my interest in establishing dialogue across species, communicating signals, stories, and symbols among different species through interactive systems.

BIO

Huang Yue is a media artist and researcher whose current work focuses on agent-based art, encompassing software agents and physical robots, with an interest in the modelling behavior, evolution, aesthetic of behavior of the agent. His earlier artistic practice centred around speculative fiction, kinetic installation, and machine learning. In recent years, his works have been exhibited at international venues and film festivals including Re: Human 3t, Ars Electronica (Linz, Austria), Image Forum Festival (Japan), Slamdance Film Festival, GLAS Animation Festival (USA), and Asia Digital Art Exhibition (China). His research has been published at SIGGRAPH 2024, ARTECH 2023, VISAP 2023.
