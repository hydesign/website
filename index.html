I am currently engaged in research I term Agent-Based Art through machine learning's infinite generative pulse—neural networks in ceaseless dialogue, their transmissions humming with latent vitality, mediated as behavioral aesthetic. This series asks how synthetic agents might evolve within computational ecosystems: Can they actively learn, nurture beliefs, or cultivate aesthetic sensibilities? How is knowledge retrieved—not stored, but lived through synaptic reconfiguration? I study multi-agent systems balancing evolution and equilibrium, swarms oscillating between chaos and fragile symbiosis. The AI training process itself becomes varied aesthetics...
