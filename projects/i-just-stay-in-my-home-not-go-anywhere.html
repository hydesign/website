I Just Stay in My Home, Not Go Anywhere

2021 1'33" Video

Starting with a deserted 3D project file, I envisage a kind of software materialism that is neither the physical reality we live in nor the virtual world of perfectly rendered results, but a ghost floating between the two realms as a half-finished product. The interface of software is revealed here. And the consciousness that lives here does not care about the crudeness of the world.
