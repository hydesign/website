Buffer Beach: Let the Currents Render is the sequel to I Just Stay in My Home, Not Go Anywhere.

Welcome to the unrendered coastline: Sand grains cling to their polygonal tempers from the modeling phase. You float on tides governed by software parameters, while the screen projecting a "real" beach is just another exhausted layer of texture. An old man dives in — his body dissolving into wireframe contours — riding glitching waves through machine-learned debris: coral reefs tessellated by default presets, jellyfish looping in half-baked animations, their tentacles flickering into fractal dust...
